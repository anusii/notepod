Checkout Notepod app, an example app built in Flutter with the Solid specification, which allows you to create, read, write and share notes. Using Notepod, you will have sovereignty (ownership and control) of your data. Your data will only be stored in your personal online data store (also called Pod or data vault). When you open the app, you can register to create your Pod, which is where your data is stored. Please check you can create a Pod, or access your existing Pod, and create, read, edit and share notes.
