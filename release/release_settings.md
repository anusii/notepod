## Promotional text

Notepod - Private and Shareable Notes

Notepod is an app built with the Solid (Social Linked Data) specification which stores your data in your own personal data vault. Unlike conventional apps, you own your data created in this app. We, the developers of the Notepod app, own only the app. We do not own the notes data that you create and save with this app. Notepod helps you set up your personal data vault, by creating an account on a Solid server host that stores personal data vaults.

Advantages:

- All your data is encrypted and you have sovereignty of your data.
- Only you (and anyone you share a note to) can read your data.
- The app developers and Solid server IT staff cannot read your data.
- You can move your data to another Solid server host.
- You can access your data from other Solid based apps.
- No advertising and no tracking, as your data is privately stored in your data vault.

Features:

- Create new notes.
- Edit an existing note.
- Share notes to anyone.
- Control who can access each note and the type of access given.
- Revoke another user's access to your note.
- Notes use standard non-proprietary Markdown formatting, with a interactive WISYG interace to assist new users.

## General description

The notepod app is an app written in Flutter with the Solid specification providing you with full sovereignty (ownership and control) of your notes. Using Notepod you can read, write, and share encrypted notes stored on your personal online data store (Pod) hosted on a Solid Server.
